Cookies Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide analytical information to website owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

inventislink.org workers.org uses cookies strictly to enhance your browsing experience, guarantee technical site stability, and remember essential interface preferences.

  • Essential / Technical Cookies: Necessary for core functionalities such as navigation, responsiveness, and secure form submissions.
  • Performance & Optimization: Helps us deliver assets quickly, cache static resources, and maintain smooth animations.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser settings to decline cookies or alert you when a cookie is placed. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain visual animations or contact form capabilities.

4. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Cookies Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or operational practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
